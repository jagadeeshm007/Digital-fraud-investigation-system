# Stakeholder Map: Digital Fraud Investigation System

## Key Stakeholders and Interests

| Stakeholder | Interest | Source |
|-------------|----------|--------|
| Hackathon organiser and judges | Observable application behaviour, hidden tests, the evaluation rubric | [Q6] |
| Fraud analysts, investigators and supervisors (end users) | Fast, explainable alerts and a clear case queue | [Q6] |

## Decision-Makers vs. Influencers

| Party | Role in scope and priority decisions | Source |
|-------|--------------------------------------|--------|
| The team | Decision-maker: decides scope and priority jointly | [Q7] |
| Hackathon organiser and judges | Influencer: the evaluation rubric, level briefs, briefing answers and hidden tests shape what the team prioritises | [Q14] |
| Azentio | Influencer: fitting Azentio's existing products (integrating, not replacing) shapes design choices | [Q15] |
